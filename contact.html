GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Visit the Shop page, choose your product, size and color, and add it to your bag. Complete the checkout form, copy the order summary, and send it to the shop through Messenger or WhatsApp.

Can I pay directly on the website?

No. This demo website doesn't process payments. The shop confirms availability and shares payment instructions after receiving your message.

Are delivery fees included?

No. The displayed subtotal excludes delivery charges. The shop will confirm the final amount.

Can I choose a different size or color?

Yes. Available sizes and colors can be selected in each product's quick-view window before adding to the bag.

How do I know my order went through?

Your order is not submitted until you paste the summary into Messenger or WhatsApp and send it. Wait for the shop to confirm your order.
